Delete FigDock Account & Personal Data

In compliance with Google Play Developer Policies and the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR Article 17 "Right to Erasure"), you have the absolute right to request the permanent deletion of your FigDock account and personal identity at any time, without reinstalling or opening the mobile application.

Irreversible Action: Account deletion permanently destroys your authentication credentials, login email, Firebase identity, user profile, and active sessions. Offline encrypted drafts on your physical device are removed on app uninstall or sign-out.

Required Information & Security Precautions

  • Required Identifier: You must specify the registered email address used to create and sign in to your FigDock account.
  • What NOT to Send: For your security, never send passwords, PINs, authentication tokens, financial account numbers, credit card details, or transaction receipts via email. FigDock staff will never ask for your password.
  • Unauthenticated Requests: We do not accept unauthenticated form submissions that claim to delete accounts without proof of ownership. All email deletion requests are subject to challenge-response verification.

How Identity is Securely Verified

To protect user accounts from unauthorized termination by third parties:

  1. If your deletion request originates directly from your registered FigDock account email, our privacy team sends a signed confirmation challenge back to that address.
  2. If your request originates from an alternate email address, the confirmation challenge is dispatched exclusively to the registered account email address on file.
  3. Deletion is scheduled only after confirmation is received from the registered mailbox. Challenges expire after 14 calendar days if unconfirmed.

Processing Timeline (Not Instantaneous)

Account deletion is an irreversible security and legal procedure and is not instantaneous:

  • Intake & Acknowledgement: Within 48 business hours of receiving your request.
  • Identity Verification Challenge: Dispatched within 3 business days.
  • Fulfillment & Erasure: Completed within 72 hours of receiving your confirmed verification.
  • Statutory Deadline: All requests are fulfilled well within the statutory ceiling of one calendar month (30 days) under UK GDPR Article 12(3).
Sole Administrator Safeguard:
If your account is the sole active administrator of an active company workspace with active team members, organizational governance rules prevent the workspace from being orphaned. You will be requested to appoint another administrator in the FigDock app or confirm closure of the workspace before personal account erasure can execute.

What Data is Deleted vs. Retained

1. Personal Data Permanently Erased:

  • Firebase Authentication identity, User ID (UID), and password credentials.
  • User profile details (display name, email address, role tags).
  • Active device session tokens and push notification registrations.
  • Pending invitations and manager branch assignments.
  • Local unsubmitted drafts on your physical device (purged upon sign-out or uninstall).

2. Business Financial Records Legally Retained:

Statutory Retention Notice (UK GDPR Article 17(3)(b)):
Under Section 388 of the United Kingdom Companies Act 2006 (s. 388(4)(a) 3-year mandatory retention for private companies; s. 388(4)(b) 6-year retention for public companies) and HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) tax regulations (6-year retention for VAT Act 1994 and Corporation Tax), business expense entries, sales records, and approval audit histories submitted on behalf of a registered company belong legally to that organization and cannot be erased.

Automated Pseudonymization: Upon account deletion, your author attribution on all retained company business records is permanently and irreversibly pseudonymized to "Former Member". Monetary amounts, timestamps, and approval decisions remain immutable for audit compliance.

In-App Deletion Alternative (For App Users)

If you currently have the FigDock Android application installed on your device, you can use the self-service flow:

  1. Open the FigDock application on your Android device.
  2. Navigate to More → Workspace & Settings → Privacy & Account Lifecycle.
  3. Tap Request Account Deletion and confirm your request.
