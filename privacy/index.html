This Privacy Policy explains our data collection, lawful processing bases, cloud infrastructure architecture, statutory financial record retention schedules, and user rights under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018, and Google Play Developer Policies.

Application Scope: FigDock Android Client (Application ID: app.figdock.financial), backend services, and official documentation websites.

Data Controller Roles:

  • Data Controller for Account Data: FigDock (published by [Pending Formal Corporate Entity Registration]) acts as the Data Controller responsible for personal account credentials (name, email address, and authentication identifiers).
  • Data Processor for Company Workspace Data: When you record business sales, expense claims, working duration, custom form responses, and operational metrics on behalf of your employer or client ("Company Workspace"), your employer is the Data Controller of that business data, and FigDock acts as a Data Processor under UK GDPR Article 28.

2. Data We Collect & Process

FigDock strictly adheres to the principle of data minimization under UK GDPR Article 5(1)(c). We only collect information essential to delivering our business financial tracking services:

2.1 Personal Identification & Authentication

  • Name: Displayed to identify you to coworkers, managers, and administrators within your company workspace.
  • Email Address: Used for authentication, password recovery, administrative alerts, and workspace invitations.
  • User Identifier (UID): Unique pseudonymous alphanumeric key assigned by Firebase Authentication for relational database integrity.

2.2 Business & Financial Operational Records

  • Financial Transactions: Business expenses, sales entries, and operational records submitted on behalf of your company (stored as exact 64-bit integer minor currency units, such as cents or pence).
  • Operational Metrics: Working duration, branch location assignments, custom form responses, and category tags.
  • Workflow Audit Trails: Administrative approval or rejection decisions, timestamps, and revision histories.

2.3 Local Device Storage (Android)

  • Encrypted Offline Drafts: Unsubmitted drafts and queued submissions stored locally on your physical Android device are encrypted at rest using SQLCipher AES-256-GCM. Cryptographic keys are protected using the Android Keystore system.

2.4 Data We Explicitly NEVER Collect

In accordance with our strict data minimization architecture:

  • We do not collect precise or approximate GPS location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION).
  • We do not collect or process biometric data, health data, or any special category data under UK GDPR Article 9.
  • We do not read or upload your device contacts, SMS messages, phone state, or call logs.
  • We do not access your device camera, microphone, or media files.
  • We do not track, collect, or share Google Advertising IDs (AAID).
  • We do not sell your personal or business data to data brokers, advertisers, or any third party.

4. Statutory Financial Record Retention Schedules

Statutory Retention Notice & UK GDPR Article 17(3)(b) Exemption

Under Section 388 of the United Kingdom Companies Act 2006 and HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) statutory regulations, companies are legally required to preserve accounting records:

  • Private Companies (Companies Act 2006 s.388(4)(a)): Mandatory retention for 3 years from the date on which they are made.
  • Public Companies (Companies Act 2006 s.388(4)(b)): Mandatory retention for 6 years from the date on which they are made.
  • Value Added Tax (VAT Act 1994, Sch. 11, para 6): VAT invoices, credit notes, and tax records preserved for 6 years.
  • Corporation Tax (Finance Act 1998, Sch. 18, para 21): Tax accounting records preserved for 6 years from the end of the accounting period.
  • PAYE & Wage Records (PAYE Regulations 2003, Reg 97): Payroll and employee payment records preserved for 3 years after the end of the tax year.

Under UK GDPR Article 17(3)(b), the right to erasure does not apply to the extent that processing is necessary for compliance with a legal obligation to maintain statutory accounting books.

Automated Pseudonymization: When your account is deleted, your personal credentials and authentication identities are permanently destroyed. Historical business records you submitted are retained under statutory obligation, but your personal author name is permanently and irreversibly pseudonymized to "Former Member". The monetary amounts, currencies, timestamps, and approval decisions remain immutable.

5. Account & Personal Data Deletion

You have the absolute right to delete your FigDock account and personal data at any time without fee. In compliance with Google Play Developer Policies, deletion can be initiated through two distinct channels:

Dual Deletion Channels (Google Play Compliant)

  • 1. In-App Deletion: If you have the FigDock Android application installed, open the app, navigate to More → Workspace & Settings → Privacy & Account Lifecycle, and tap Request Account Deletion. In-app deletion provides immediate authenticated identity verification and sole-administrator checks.
  • 2. External Customer Service Request (Without Installing the App): If you have uninstalled the application or cannot access your mobile device, you can submit an official deletion request via email to our customer service desk at maahithium@gmail.com. Step-by-step guidance, pre-formatted email templates, and verification requirements are detailed on our Account Deletion Portal.

5.1 The Sole Administrator Guard

If you are the sole active administrator of an active company workspace, you cannot delete your account until you appoint a replacement administrator in the FigDock app or close the company workspace. This prevents business workspaces containing active team members from becoming permanently orphaned. Authoritative sole-administrator status is determined exclusively by database relational queries against company membership structures, never through heuristic or simulated email matching.

5.2 Deletion Fulfillment SLA

Once an account deletion request is confirmed, credential invalidation occurs immediately, and complete backend fulfillment (PostgreSQL pseudonymization and Firebase Auth deletion) executes within our operational SLA window (target 72 hours; statutory ceiling of one calendar month under UK GDPR Article 12(3)).

6. Data Security & Storage Architecture

FigDock enforces defense-in-depth security safeguards across all architectural layers:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications enforce TLS 1.3 with modern cryptographic cipher suites. Cleartext HTTP traffic is prohibited in the Android Network Security Configuration.
  • Encryption at Rest (Cloud): Managed PostgreSQL databases, automated snapshots, and write-ahead logs on Google Cloud Platform use AES-256 encryption at rest.
  • Encryption at Rest (Device): Local unsubmitted drafts on your Android device are encrypted using SQLCipher AES-256-GCM backed by hardware-protected keys in the Android Keystore.
  • Multi-Tenant Isolation: Company workspaces are strictly isolated at the database level through PostgreSQL Row-Level Security triggers and Data Connect authorization rules.

8. Your Legal Rights (UK GDPR & EU GDPR)

Under applicable data protection legislation, you possess the following rights:

  • Right of Access (Art. 15): You have the right to request confirmation of whether we process your personal data and to obtain a copy.
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16): You have the right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal details.
  • Right to Erasure / "Right to be Forgotten" (Art. 17): You have the right to request account deletion and data erasure, subject to statutory accounting retention exemptions under Article 17(3)(b).
  • Right to Restrict Processing (Art. 18): You have the right to restrict processing under certain conditions.
  • Right to Data Portability (Art. 20): You have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object (Art. 21): You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests.

To exercise any of these statutory rights, please contact our customer support and privacy desk at maahithium@gmail.com.
