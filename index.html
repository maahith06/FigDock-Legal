Architectural Principles & Privacy By Design

  • Data Minimization: FigDock collects only what is essential for application functionality (user display name, email, authentication UID, and company operational records).
  • Zero Tracking: FigDock does not track GPS location, read device contacts, access SMS messages, record microphone audio, or collect Google Advertising IDs (AAID).
  • Cryptographic Device Security: Unsubmitted local offline drafts on your Android device are encrypted at rest using SQLCipher AES-256-GCM backed by hardware-protected keys in the Android Keystore.
  • Encryption in Transit: TLS 1.3 is strictly enforced for all network communications. Cleartext HTTP is prohibited in the Android Network Security Configuration.
  • Statutory Record Retention: Under Section 388 of the United Kingdom Companies Act 2006 and HMRC rules, company business accounting records belong legally to the employer organization and are preserved with author attribution permanently pseudonymized to "Former Member" upon account deletion.
